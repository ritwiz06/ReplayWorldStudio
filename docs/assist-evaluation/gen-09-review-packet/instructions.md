# Reviewing 25 before-and-after pictures

Thank you for helping. This takes about 15 to 20 minutes.

## What you are looking at

Someone asked a 3D modelling tool to change a simple model of a piece of furniture or equipment.
Each sheet shows:

- the request, in one line at the top;
- **Before:** the model as it was;
- **After:** the model the tool proposed, as the person would see it before accepting it.

The models are deliberately simple blocks and shapes. Judge whether the **requested change** can
be seen, not whether the model looks realistic.

Glass and clear plastic are drawn see-through with blue edge lines. An **orange outline** marks
a part the tool itself flagged as a possible problem. An outline only shows where something
is: if you cannot see the part itself, it does not count as visible.

## What to do

1. Open `sheets/` and look at each sheet at 100 % zoom or larger, in any order.
2. For each sheet, fill in one row of `answers-template.csv` (any spreadsheet program works),
   using the rubric in `rubric.md`:
   - `score`: 0, 1 or 2;
   - `looks_done_but_is_not`: yes or no;
   - `note`: optional, one short sentence.
3. Judge from the pictures alone. Please do not look anything up, ask anyone, or open other
   files in this project. Nothing else in the packet tells you the right answer.
4. Send the filled-in file back to the person who gave you the packet.

There are no trick sheets, and there is no time limit. If you are unsure, pick the closer
score and say why in the note.

## What is recorded

Only your answers file and the short description of you that you agree with the coordinator
(for example "colleague, not involved in the project"). No name or contact details go into the
project's files unless you ask for them to.
