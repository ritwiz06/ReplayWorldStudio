## Stable context

- Product: AI-assisted platform for realistic, editable worlds from drawings, photos, videos and specifications. Factory planning is the **first example** of a general editable-world platform (ADR-030).
- Current priority: guided creation for people without CAD skills (M2), then one image to an editable world (M3), then drawing import (M4).
- First application: factory production-area planning (one small area, ~5-20 equipment instances, one process family).
- Broad platform scope (photo/video, physics, engineering, aging, teams) remains on the roadmap; see EXPANSION_ROADMAP.md.
- Previous HTML concept and planning PDF: not present in this repository; not required.
